How your answer is checked
A formula is shown with one side missing — type the other side.
· Powers: a^2 or a² — both work.
· Division: a/b or a÷b — both work.
· Multiplication: × or * or nothing at all — A×(a+b), A*(a+b) and A(a+b) all work.
· Term order never matters — write the terms in any order, any number of ways: a²+2ab+b², b²+a²+2ab, 2ab+b²+a² … all count as correct, as long as the terms themselves are right.
· Checking is approximate. The correct answer is always shown after you submit, so you can judge for yourself too.
